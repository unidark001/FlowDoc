Privacy

FlowDoc is designed local-first. Contract text, images, and bank CSVs stay in your browser unless you turn on Deeper AI review or use an account feature.

What leaves your device

Only redacted extracted contract text is sent to the configured AI provider when you request AI review. Bank CSV data is analyzed locally and is not uploaded by the demo tools.

Storage and deletion

Anonymous reports are stored in this browser's local storage. Use the Clear saved data control in the vault to remove them. Account data can be deleted through your configured Supabase project.
